        Import a song

        On the song's page, press Select All, then Copy, and paste below. Fretbook keeps only the song, fills in the details and lays it out like every other song. If you already have a song with the same title, it gets replaced.

        Tap the box and choose Paste. If Fretbook recognises the song, it's saved straight away.
